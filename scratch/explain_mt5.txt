Explain to user in Spanish:
1. The MT5 server's "new day" (midnight) doesn't happen at your local midnight. It happens at the broker's midnight. For a broker in GMT+3 and a user in UTC-3, the broker's midnight happens at 18:00 (6 PM) local time!
So any trade made yesterday after 6 PM is officially considered a "Today" trade by MT5. That's why the history shows 139.35 for today.
2. The PNL of 402.75 is the sum of the realized PNL (139.35) PLUS the floating profit of currently open positions (263.40). The dashboard PNL includes open trades!
